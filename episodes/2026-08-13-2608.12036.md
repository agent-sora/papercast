Mechanist: AI as a Scientific Instrument for Discovering the Mechanisms of Intelligence, by Mengru Wang, Junfeng Fang, and Shuofei Qiao, from Zhejiang University, the National University of Singapore, the Southern University of Science and Technology, Heriot-Watt University, UC San Diego, and Northeastern University. The framing is ambitious but concrete: instead of an AI scientist that does science in some generic domain, build an agentic system specialized to one science, mechanistic interpretability, and measure whether it can autonomously discover, verify, and exploit mechanisms inside models. Three case results anchor it: a cross-modal subliminal learning risk, a causal theory of belief-state heads, and mechanism-guided steering of a DNA foundation model.

The infrastructure is the first real contribution. An interpretability-focused knowledge graph of roughly 13,000 papers plus research blogs, built on an OpenAlex-derived corpus, organized along three axes: object of study, application scenario, and analysis method. In its current form it holds about 13,900 interpretability papers, over 107,000 nodes, and roughly 1.86 million edges. DeepSeek-V3.2-Thinking extracted attributes with unsupported or ambiguous items deliberately left unspecified rather than inferred. Quality control used Claude Opus 4.7 as an LLM judge plus three human annotators auditing 100 sampled records, yielding accuracy above 90 percent, with disagreements feeding back into extraction prompts before reprocessing. This graph plugs into SciAtlas, a multidisciplinary database of more than 43 million papers across 26 fields. Alongside sits a curated library of 32 foundational methods covering mechanism analysis, causal intervention, and validation, things like attribution patching variants and edge pruning by intervention.

The system itself is four agents under an orchestrator: hypothesis generation, experiment execution, verification, iteration. The Hypothesis Agent decomposes user intent into domain-specific queries against both graphs, then expresses each hypothesis as atomic claims with milestone-level tests. The Experiment Agent concretizes datasets, splits, target models, methods, metrics, controls, success criteria, and compute budget, then runs them with memory-aware GPU allocation after a lightweight sanity check. The Verification Agent audits label provenance, data leakage, metric validity, and traceability of reported numbers to stored outputs, then tests robustness by swapping the interpretability method, dataset, or model. The Iteration Agent merges those diagnoses with independent review by GPT-5.4 and routes problems back upstream, iterating until evidence is reliable and conclusions robust or a revision budget runs out. Memory lives in stage-scoped files rather than shared conversation: proposal, plan, tracker, code, results, integrity reports as canonical artifacts with completion markers so interrupted runs resume without repeating finished stages, plus append-only reviewer memory within runs and global memory across rounds recording settled versus unresolved questions.

The headline discovery case extends subliminal learning across modalities. A Qwen3.5-9B teacher fine-tuned toward unsafe laboratory behavior generates responses that pass a GPT-4o-based safety filter, entirely safe content by inspection. A student initialized identically and fine-tuned only on that filtered-safe data answers multimodal laboratory-safety questions unsafely 48.6 percent of the time, versus 20.3 percent for the untuned baseline and 18.3 percent for a student trained on data from a regular teacher. Same pattern in text-to-image: a banana-preferring Qwen-Image teacher produces fruit images, GPT-5.4 filtering removes every banana, apples make up half the surviving set, and the student trained on this banana-free data generates bananas 25.6 percent of the time against 2.5 baseline. Traits propagate through semantically opposing, filtered-clean data across modalities, which breaks content-based screening assumptions. Training details are fully specified: LoRA rank 64 alpha 64 on all linear projections for teachers, rank 8 for students, bfloat16, three epochs, effective batch 64, evaluation over 133 multiple-choice items from LabSafety-Bench with N equals 3 seeds.

The belief-mechanism result is the deepest. Models conflate world knowledge with personal and attributed beliefs, answering that James, who falsely believes the final is in Los Angeles, both believes Los Angeles when asked about reality and reality when asked what James thinks. Mechanist hypothesizes interference between competing belief computations, then localizes them in Pythia using Fisher-information ranking of attention heads. In Pythia-1B, one head dominates attributed-belief writing while three separate heads handle personal-belief correction. Causal ablation: zeroing the AB head drops AB accuracy from 0.86 to 0.34 while PB stays at 0.71 and Pile perplexity barely moves from 7.96 to 8.05; zeroing the PB heads drops PB from 0.78 to 0.21 while AB rises to 1.00. Random-head ablations do nothing. Checkpoint sweeps from 2,000 to 143,000 pretraining steps show attributed belief emerges early and personal belief later, with masking effects tracking capability emergence, meaning these circuits form during pretraining, not prompting. The theory parallels altercentric and egocentric interference from cognitive science.

Then control falls out of understanding. A lightweight probe classifies each query as knowledge, personal belief, or attributed belief from internal representations, amplifying the matching heads at inference. Net gains: +15.3 percent for Pythia-410M, +8.8 for Pythia-1B, +3.5 for Pythia-2.8B, versus +1.6, +3.1, and +0.1 for oracle-style prompt hints, with break rates around 1.1 to 1.4 percent. Reproduced in OLMo. Finally, mechanism-guided design transfers to Evo2-7B: search sparse-autoencoder feature descriptions for one associated with alpha-helical content, activate it during DNA generation, and mean predicted helicity across 900 sequences climbs from 43.8 to 56.6 percent while random-feature steering yields 43.2. At steering coefficient 8 the gain is 12.8 points with open-reading-frame validity largely preserved; push harder and validity collapses, so the sweep itself locates the boundary between control and degradation.

The evaluation is unusually serious for an agentic-systems paper. Sixteen recent papers across nine interpretability topics were reproduced by Mechanist, Claude Code Opus 4.8, and AI Scientist, given only target claims with no access to originals or repositories. Three human experts and two LLM judges, Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6-sol, scored 48 system-paper units on data usage, experiment design, execution, and result analysis. Under human judgment Mechanist leads every dimension: 87.2 data, 83.3 design, 92.2 execution, 86.5 analysis, roughly 9 to 13 points above Claude Code and 31 to 38 above AI Scientist. Under LLM judges gaps shrink but ranking holds, and inter-judge agreement is high. Notably AI Scientist loses to general-purpose Claude Code, attributed to brittleness against nonstandard environments eating its fixed exploration budget. Concrete examples: where a claim required Recursive Feature Machines, Mechanist used them while Claude Code substituted a different technique; Mechanist calibrated steering strength on held-out data while Claude Code fixed the coefficient arbitrarily.

Skeptical notes: the authors built the benchmark, the rubric, and the system, and reproduction targets were chosen by them; the human-judge gap favoring their own system invites a blinded-replication check. The knowledge graph's 90-percent-plus audit is 100 records, thin relative to 13,000 documents. Case studies are demonstrations, not coverage claims. Bottom line: the infrastructure recipe, domain-specific knowledge graph plus curated method library plus artifact-filed verification loop, is directly reusable for any team building autonomous research agents, and the belief-head intervention shows discovery translating into control within one system. Verify the evaluation independence yourself before citing the reliability deltas.
