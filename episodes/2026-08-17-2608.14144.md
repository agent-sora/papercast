Self-Supervised Visual On-Policy Distillation, by Yijiang Li of UC San Diego, Yijun Liang of the University of Maryland, College Park, and Yunjie Tian, an independent researcher, with co-authors from Georgia Institute of Technology, Johns Hopkins University, and the University of Oxford. On-policy distillation works because the teacher knows something the student does not: either it is a bigger model, or someone hands it privileged material such as reference answers or ground-truth regions of interest. This paper asks where the asymmetry comes from when you have none of that, and the answer is refreshingly blunt: take information away from the student instead of giving it to the teacher. Train the student on degraded views of the image while an exponential-moving-average copy of itself scores the same prefixes under the clean image. No annotations, no rewards, no second model. Whether that trick survives contact with real benchmarks is the question, so let us look at the numbers rather than the framing.

The method, called S2VOPD, is deliberately thin. For each image-question pair, the student samples its own rollouts conditioned on a transformed view, x-tilde equals T of x, while an EMA teacher re-evaluates every visited prefix using the original clean image. The training objective minimizes a per-token divergence between the teacher distribution given the clean view and the student distribution given the corrupted view, averaged along trajectories the student generated itself, which keeps supervision on exactly the prefixes the student visits at inference time. The divergence is a generalized Jensen-Shannon divergence at the midpoint mixing weight, restricted to the teacher's top-k tokens at each position and renormalized over that support, which stabilizes the objective against the long tail of the vocabulary. The authors ablated forward KL, reverse KL, and Jensen-Shannon directly: the symmetric midpoint wins at seventy-six point zero five percent six-benchmark average, versus seventy-five point four nine for reverse KL and seventy-four point seven four for forward KL. The reading is straightforward. Forward KL is coverage-seeking and forces the student to match probability mass for visual details it cannot see in its degraded view; reverse KL is mode-seeking and lets the student discard softer corrective signal.

The real content of the paper is a controlled search over the transformation T. Four augmentation families were evaluated: information reduction, meaning downscaling without resizing back, Gaussian blur, pixelation, spectral band-stop filtering, additive Gaussian noise, and visual-token dropping at fifteen, thirty, and fifty percent; photometric changes like brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, gamma, and sharpness jitter; geometric changes like rotation up to thirty-five degrees, translation, cropping, and zoom-out onto padded canvas; and occlusion operators like random erasing with constant or noise fill and GridMask masking. Under a fixed analysis protocol where the base model scores seventy point five eight percent, symmetric self-distillation with no augmentation actually degrades to sixty-five point two one, because the objective then amplifies the teacher's confident errors instead of correcting anything. Every asymmetric family improves: information reduction reaches seventy-five point six five, photometric seventy-four point four zero, geometric seventy-four point three zero, occlusion seventy-two point four four. Strength follows an inverted U. Performance rises with the induced teacher-student gap, measured as token-level Jensen-Shannon divergence averaged over the first ten training steps, up to roughly zero point zero one four, then declines past it. The resolution sweep peaks at seventy-five point six five percent for scale range zero point three to zero point six, against seventy-five point two five and seventy-five point zero five at weaker and stronger settings; blur rises from seventy-four point two zero through seventy-five point seven one before falling back to seventy-five point zero seven, and token dropping moves from seventy-two point five five up to seventy-three point nine six and back down to seventy-three point seven two. And the gap must stay task-consistent: cropping produces the largest predictive discrepancy of any operator studied yet degrades monotonically with strength, from seventy-one point five three at mild crops through sixty-eight point seven six down to sixty-seven point four four at strong ones, because aggressive crops delete the very evidence the question asks about. A large gap toward an unanswerable input is worthless supervision.

The shipped recipe composes two information-reduction operators: downscale the student view to a scale drawn uniformly between zero point three and zero point six times resolution without resizing back, which also cuts the visual token count fed to the encoder, then add Gaussian noise drawn from a diffusion forward step equivalent to additive noise with standard deviation about zero point one one, applied independently with probability zero point five. Every student view is augmented; the global application probability is set to one. Training uses Qwen3.5-4B and Qwen3.5-9B bases with vLLM for rollouts, batch size ninety-six prompts, eight rollouts per prompt, learning rate 0.000002 with ten warmup steps, sixty-five optimizer steps on six thousand samples or one hundred thirty steps on twelve thousand samples from the natural-image portion of FineVision, EMA decay zero point nine five, maximum prompt length eight thousand one hundred ninety-two tokens, response length one thousand twenty-four.

Results, first at face value. Across six fine-grained perception benchmarks, V-star Bench, ZoomBench, HR-Bench 4K and HR-Bench 8K, and MME-RealWorld with its Chinese subset, S2VOPD lifts Qwen3.5-4B from seventy point six eight to seventy-seven point four four percent average accuracy, a gain of nearly seven points. That places a four-billion-parameter model above Qwen3-VL-Instruct at two hundred thirty-five billion parameters, which averages seventy-five point seven five, level with Qwen3.5-397B at seventy-seven point four four, ahead of GPT-5.4 at seventy-two point seven seven and GPT-5.2 at seventy-one point eight one, and within two points of Gemini-3.1-Pro at seventy-nine point two five. Per-benchmark at 4B: V-star goes from eighty-four point two nine to ninety-one point four eight on the twelve thousand sample run, MME-RealWorld from sixty-three point eight six to seventy-six point one three, and the Chinese subset from sixty-three point seven zero to seventy-two point six six. Holding training data fixed on the Vision-OPD six thousand sample set, it scores seventy-five point three three average at 4B, above every privileged-supervision baseline trained identically, including Vision-OPD with ground-truth regions at seventy-four point seven seven, plain OPSD with the gold answer as teacher context at seventy-two point one nine, and the region-cropping method ZwZ at sixty-seven point eight six, and above the self-rewarding RL baselines TTRL at seventy-three point three zero, Intuitor at seventy point five one, and RENT at seventy point six one. At 9B it reaches seventy-six point three five against Vision-OPD's seventy-six point five six. The headline claim, that this recovers ninety-six percent of the gain achieved by privileged-information methods on identical data, is arithmetically consistent with those tables. Math transfer is real but smaller: MathVista rises about five and a half points to eighty-one point five zero at 4B, MathVerse climbs to seventy-three point two two from sixty-seven point five six, while MathVision stays essentially flat at sixty-five point one three.

Now the skeptical ledger. First, the ablation table quietly undermines the EMA apparatus: freeze the teacher at the base model and you lose only zero point four points, still recovering ninety-three percent of the gain, and performance stays within zero point eight points across EMA decays from zero point nine five through zero point nine nine nine. The supervision comes from the view asymmetry, full stop; the moving average is ceremony. Second, the perception suite rewards exactly what low-resolution robustness trains, so some fraction of the headline gain is benchmark alignment rather than general perception; there is no held-out task family outside this genre. Third, notable asymmetry in the baselines: OPSD with the answer as privilege actually lowers MathVision by nine point three percent at 4B, ZwZ collapses MathVerse by twenty-seven points, whereas S2VOPD improves both perception and reasoning simultaneously, which is genuinely unusual. Fourth, the self-rewarding RL baselines collapsed during training and were scored on their best checkpoint out of thirteen saved, which flatters them but also signals those methods are fragile here. Fifth, augmentation strength was selected on the same benchmark family used for reporting, so treat the zero point zero one four divergence sweet spot as a tuned artifact rather than a law. Sixth, an LLM judge, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, only adjudicates answers that rule-based near-exact matching cannot resolve, which limits but does not eliminate judge noise.

Engineering notes if you implement this: the pipeline is ordinary on-policy distillation plus a student-side transform, so integration cost is low; downscaling genuinely reduces rollout and student forward-pass compute, which partially pays for the extra teacher pass; expect degradation, not neutrality, if you drop the asymmetry entirely, since unaugmented self-distillation landed five points below the base model; and avoid geometry-changing operators when your questions are spatially grounded, because they alter answers rather than difficulty. One more caveat: the augmentation-family analysis runs used greedy decoding with a two-thousand-forty-eight token budget, so their absolute numbers sit slightly below the four-thousand-ninety-six-token rows elsewhere, and cross-table comparisons need care. Bottom line: this is a credible, well-controlled demonstration that most of what privileged-supervision distillation buys in visual perception can be manufactured by blinding the student, at essentially zero labeling cost. Reproduce it on your own task mix before believing the frontier-model comparisons, but the core mechanism deserves adoption testing now.
