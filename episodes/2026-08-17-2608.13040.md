Latent On-Policy Self-Distillation, by Guibin Zhang of the National University of Singapore, Jiayang Lyu of Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, and Ran Sun, with co-authors from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. The paper addresses the central design question in self-evolving agents: when a model teaches itself from experience, what should the teacher be allowed to see? On-policy self-distillation answers this today with designer-specified artifacts. The teacher gets verified reasoning traces, or final answers, or environment feedback, or successful sibling rollouts, or retrieved documents, and each variant works somewhere and fails somewhere else. LOPD's move is structural rather than incremental: stop hand-designing the privilege entirely. Compose retrieved raw trajectories into continuous latent tokens, make that composition learnable end-to-end alongside the student, and let gradient descent decide what experience is worth encoding for supervision. After training you throw away everything except the student.

The mechanics, concretely. An experience bank stores successful rollouts offline as task descriptions plus compact action-result traces with verbose observations stripped. At training time a dense retriever fetches entries by cosine similarity over task-trace pairs, defaulting to three. A composer then processes each retrieved item: an encoder with trainable LoRA adapters over a frozen backbone maps the task-experience pair into hidden states, and a QFormer-style cross-attention compressor with learned queries squeezes variable-length states into exactly thirty-two continuous latent tokens per experience, ninety-six tokens total. These embeddings enter the frozen-teacher copy's context window as ordinary positions. The student rolls out multi-turn trajectories conditioned only on the task and interaction history, capped at thirty environment steps for tool use; the teacher re-scores every visited prefix given the latent context; reverse-KL distillation matches distributions restricted to the teacher's top-twenty vocabulary entries plus one aggregated tail bucket, which keeps logit computation cheap. Gradients flow through the frozen teacher network back into the latent inputs, so the composer learns from the same backward pass that trains the student. Tool-use rollouts use the EnvScaler corpus, coding uses DeepCoder's TACO subset with decoding temperature and top-p held fixed across all methods within each benchmark, so the comparison isolates privileged-context construction and distillation loss as the only moving parts.

The failure mode this design must defeat is collapse, and the paper handles it explicitly. Nothing stops the composer from minimizing distillation loss by making the latent context uninformative, letting the teacher converge to the student and the loss to zero without any learning. Two countermeasures: cold-starting the composer on successful trajectories with the backbone frozen, and a privileged-margin constraint tying the teacher's token-level advantage to verified outcomes. Per-token privilege is defined as the teacher log-probability of the student's actual sampled token minus the student's own, computed by gathering values already produced during forward passes, no extra forwards. The outcome-weighted mean of this privilege across masked action tokens, weighted by trajectory advantage of twice reward minus one, must exceed a margin threshold m set to zero point zero five, enforced through a dual variable updated by a simple rule, plus an anchor term penalizing drift of the latent context from initialization. If the composer degenerates, privilege goes to zero, the dual penalty activates, and the trivial solution is structurally excluded. Inference deploys only the student: no retrieval module, no composer, no latents, no experience bank.

Results across two regimes. Agentic tool use trained on twenty-three hundred forty-nine EnvScaler-derived tasks, evaluated on a held-out two-hundred-task split plus BFCL-v3 across its base, missing-function, missing-parameter, and long-context subsets, plus ACEBench multi-step and multi-turn. Coding trained on seven thousand verified Python problems from the DeepCoder TACO subset with a sixteen-thousand-three-hundred-eighty-four-token response budget, evaluated on LiveCodeBench v5 and v6 plus HumanEval+ and MBPP+. Backbones are Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-8B, and Olmo3-7B, against vanilla, GRPO, Skill-SD, OPSD with oracle traces, SDPO with feedback-conditioned teachers, and SDFT with demonstration conditioning. LOPD posts the best aggregate result in all ten backbone-benchmark comparisons. With Qwen3-4B it improves EnvScaler from sixty-one point eight under GRPO to sixty-three point seven, and with Qwen3-8B it reaches sixty-six point four on EnvScaler, twenty-nine point eight eight average BFCL-v3, and sixty-two point seven on ACEBench, versus strongest-baseline marks of sixty point two, twenty-nine, and fifty-eight. On coding with Qwen3-4B it hits forty-eight point seven eight LiveCodeBench aggregate and eighty-one point three six EvalPlus average; with Olmo3-7B, fifty point nine eight and seventy-eight point four one. The baselines' instability is itself instructive: SDPO drops Qwen3-4B from twenty-two point eight eight to fifteen point seven five on BFCL-v3 and OPSD falls below vanilla on LiveCodeBench for both backbones, while OPSD simultaneously helps the long-context BFCL subset, so the same fixed context can help one regime and damage another depending on how well its construction matches the current policy's coverage. LOPD stays above vanilla in all ten settings, with gains from one point five points to seventeen point two on EnvScaler with Qwen3-8B. Sample efficiency is the sharper claim: LOPD crosses mean reward zero point six one after just three hundred twenty rollouts and reaches its plateau of roughly zero point six three seven by generation five hundred seventy-six, while GRPO and Skill-SD crawl to zero point six one one and zero point five eight eight across the full one thousand six hundred generation budget, meaning LOPD surpasses both using under thirty percent of their rollout spend.

The ablation that carries the thesis: joint optimization versus frozen composer. Training with the frozen cold-started composer yields zero point five seven three EnvScaler reward. Dropping the margin entirely degrades to zero point five five one because unconstrained distillation gradients corrupt the context. Weak margins at zero point zero one do not help. From zero point zero two upward students beat the frozen baseline, reaching zero point six zero three at m equal to zero point zero two, peaking at zero point six three seven with m equal to zero point zero five, and easing to zero point six two six at zero point one zero. So the distillation signal genuinely teaches the composer something initialization could not, but only under the outcome constraint. Capacity analysis shows a hard threshold: eight or sixteen latent tokens per experience stay near fifty-six percent reward, thirty-two jumps to sixty-three point seven, and sixty-four or one hundred twenty-eight add nothing consistent. Retrieval count saturates at three experiences, improving from zero point six zero five with one retrieval with no monotone benefit beyond. Behavioral internalization shows up in interaction patterns that persist without privileged context: relative to vanilla, the distilled student uses more environment steps, seventeen versus eleven, but far fewer speculative tool calls per step, one point one one versus three point five zero, produces a first response thirty-seven and a half percent shorter, repeats fewer calls, and earns more reward per call. Decoding attempts on the latent tokens through the language-model head produce fragmented multilingual noise whether or not tasks condition them, consistent with distributed representation rather than readable copying of retrieved procedures; the retrieved neighbors themselves were sensible, like a benefits-plan update analogue at rank two with zero point eight eight six similarity for a plan-management task, or a Josephus-recurrence match for a circular-elimination coding problem.

Skeptical notes before adoption. All benchmarks are tool-use and code domains where verifiable rewards exist; nothing here tests open-ended settings where outcome verification is itself unreliable. The margin threshold and latent-token count were tuned on EnvScaler, and the sensitivity curves show non-monotone behavior around the chosen operating points, so budget for per-domain sweeps. Teacher forward passes through a second frozen copy add training cost even though rollout counts drop. And the comparison baselines, while representative, each carry their own context-construction choices; some gaps may shrink with better hand-tuned variants, though the reversals documented for fixed contexts across regimes support the learnability argument. Bottom line: making the teacher's privilege a learned latent substrate rather than a designed artifact is a defensible step toward scalable self-improvement, backed by clean ablations and consistent wins across ten comparisons. Test the composer pipeline against your own retrieval stack first, because its value hinges on experience quality your bank may not yet have.
